Milestones Group

Milestones Group is a Value Added Distributor that enriches the Middle East & African UC market. Through our global vendors, we provide a series of the latest collaborative solutions that facilitate collaboration and communications for corporate and enterprises such as VOIP, video conferencing solutions, personal collaboration solutions, CRM, and more.

Our highly qualified team has more than 10 years of experience in unified communications. With today’s constantly changing dynamic in the UC market, we are honored to share valuable industry information with our CUSTOMERS to keep them up to date.

Our Solutions

Looking for Reliable Technology Solutions?

Choosing the right Unified Communications provider is essential for improving collaboration, enhancing productivity, and enabling seamless communication across your organization. At Milestones Group, we specialize in delivering reliable Unified Communication solutions, IP telephony systems, and collaboration technologies that help businesses connect teams, customers, and partners efficiently.


Expertise and Industry Experience

Our team of experienced professionals specializes in Unified Communications, VoIP solutions, IP telephony, and enterprise collaboration platforms. We help organizations implement modern communication technologies that streamline workflows and improve internal and external communication.

Every business has unique communication needs. At Milestones Group, we design customized Unified Communication solutions tailored to your infrastructure, operational requirements, and business objectives, ensuring scalable and future-ready communication

Reliable Support and System Performance
